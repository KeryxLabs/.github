Not faster. Not easier. **More meaningful.**

We create tools that **augment how you think instead of replacing your thinking**.
Infrastructure that makes data **legible and actionable**, not outsourced.
Software that has a **point of view**, because the people who built it had one.

We are not an AI lab.
We are a **modern data integration and reasoning lab**.
AI is a lens. Data is the substance. Humans are still the point.

Real people want AI to power them, not replace them.
**We build the exoskeleton, not the autopilot.**

## The Stack

Six layers. One coherent system. Each stands on its own — and becomes something more when composed.

### 05 · Experience

- **Medousa** — *Do the work.* A local-first AI workspace on your machines. Tell her what's weighing on you, wake up to finished guides in your vault — not chat that disappears. → [medousa.app](https://medousa.app)

- **Resonantia** — *Navigate the mind.* A local-first app for navigating your AI sessions as geometry. Explore the topology of your own thinking, rendered as a constellation across time. Not a summary — a return. → [resonantia.me](https://resonantia.me)

### 04 · Runtime

- **Stasis** — Long-running AI, finally. A Rust framework for AI systems that behave like distributed applications: durable execution, queue ownership, scheduling, retries, and runtime observability. → [runstasis.io](https://runstasis.io)

### 03 · Automation

- **Grapheme** — A governed workflow engine. Language and runtime for policy-scoped automations with explicit control flow, typed transitions, and capability-aware execution. → [github.com/EntasisLabs/grapheme](https://github.com/EntasisLabs/grapheme)

### 02 · Memory

- **Locus** — The architectural realization of a memory palace. Stitches AVEC, STTP, and retrieval into persistent memory composites. Build extensive memory layouts, the way you envision them. → [locus.black](https://locus.black)

### 01 · Protocols

- **STTP** (Spatio-Temporal Transfer Protocol) — the typed IR language for cognition. Enables A2A quasi-deterministic communication and verification via structure.

- **ACC** (Adaptive Codec Context) — models **codebases as living systems**, quantifying stability, logic, friction, and autonomy, and providing **actionable insight** directly in your editor.

- **DMC** (Design Meaning Context) — STTP's little cousin for running group studies across different AVEC profiles. STTP keeps what remains true; DMC emerges the meaning behind what was said.

- **instrumenta** — protocol-first open tooling for persistent AI memory (STTP) and adaptive code intelligence (ACC). The machinery, not vague magic. → [github.com/EntasisLabs/instrumenta](https://github.com/EntasisLabs/instrumenta)

### 00 · Foundation

- **AVEC** (Attractor Vector Encoding Configuration) — the dimensional coordinate system everything else speaks. An OCEAN-inspired take (the Big 5 of psychology) applied to AI context, mapping reasoning posture across **Stability, Logic, Friction, and Autonomy**.
